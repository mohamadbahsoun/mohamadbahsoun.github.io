Drupal Business Website

A structured and responsive business website built around Drupal CMS, demonstrating content management, reusable components, custom configuration and integration with business systems.

Project Overview

This reference project represents a typical company website where content editors need to manage website information through Drupal while keeping the presentation and application logic maintainable.

Business Scenario

A company requires a professional website for presenting its services, company information, news and customer contact options.

CMS Solution

Drupal provides the content management platform, allowing authorized users to maintain structured website content.

Content Management

Content types and reusable components allow editors to maintain pages without changing the underlying website code.

Custom Development

Drupal can be extended with custom modules, integrations and application-specific functionality when required.
