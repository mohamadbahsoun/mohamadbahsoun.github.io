SharePoint Intranet & Portal Development

Designing and developing a centralized SharePoint-based intranet portal for company information, documents, departments, forms, announcements and employee resources.

Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates how a SharePoint intranet can be designed to provide employees with a centralized location for company information, documents, policies, departmental resources and internal business processes.

Business Scenario

A company needs an internal portal where employees can quickly access company news, policies, procedures, forms, departmental information and frequently used resources.

Business Objective

Create a structured and easy-to-navigate intranet that centralizes information while providing appropriate access to departments and employees.

Primary Users

Employees, department managers, HR, Finance, Sales, IT and management.

Platform

Microsoft SharePoint with structured sites, pages, document libraries, lists, permissions and navigation.

Business Requirements

The portal was designed around common requirements found in business intranet environments.

  • Centralized company information
  • Company news and announcements
  • Department-specific information
  • Central document management
  • Policies and procedures
  • Employee forms and requests
  • Quick links to business resources
  • Controlled access to sensitive information
  • Searchable documents and information
  • Simple and consistent navigation

Intranet Features

The portal can provide a range of features to improve internal communication and document accessibility.

Company News

Centralized announcements and company news allow employees to stay informed about important organizational updates.

Document Libraries

Policies, procedures, templates and other business documents are organized into searchable SharePoint document libraries.

Department Pages

Dedicated areas can provide each department with its own information, documents, links and resources.

Forms & Requests

Internal requests such as IT support, vacation requests and purchase requests can be organized using SharePoint lists and forms.

Quick Links

Frequently used internal applications, resources and websites can be exposed through centralized quick links.

Search & Navigation

Structured navigation and SharePoint search help employees locate documents and information quickly.

Document Management

The intranet uses SharePoint document libraries to provide structured storage for common business documents.

Policies

Company policies can be organized by category and made available to employees according to their access permissions.

Procedures

Operational procedures can be maintained in a centralized and searchable location.

Templates

Standard business templates can be maintained so employees always have access to approved versions.

Version Management

SharePoint document management capabilities can help maintain document history and controlled collaboration.

Permissions & Security

Access should be designed according to business roles and information sensitivity.

Employee Access

General company information and employee resources can be available to the appropriate employee groups.

Department Access

Department-specific resources can be restricted to the appropriate teams or groups.

Management Access

Sensitive management information can be protected through appropriate SharePoint permissions.

Permission Strategy

Access should be planned around groups and roles rather than relying on unnecessary individual permissions.

Implementation Approach

Requirements Analysis

Identify business departments, users, documents, processes and information that need to be included in the intranet.

Information Architecture

Define the site structure, navigation, pages, libraries, lists and departmental areas.

SharePoint Configuration

Configure sites, pages, libraries, lists, permissions and navigation.

Content Organization

Organize documents, policies, procedures, templates and internal resources.

Testing

Validate navigation, permissions, document access, forms and search functionality.

Deployment & Maintenance

Deploy the completed portal and establish a process for ongoing content and permission management.

Business Benefits

Centralized Information

Employees have a central location for important company information and resources.

Improved Accessibility

Employees can find documents, forms and resources without searching across multiple locations.

Better Organization

Standardized information architecture provides a consistent structure for business content.

Controlled Access

Business information can be organized according to departments, teams and user responsibilities.
