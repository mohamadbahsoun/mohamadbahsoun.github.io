Umbraco Business Website

A responsive business website built around the Umbraco CMS, with structured content, reusable page components, navigation, contact content and an administration-friendly content model.

Project Overview

This reference project represents a typical company website where business users need to manage website content through a CMS rather than modifying application code for every content change.

Business Scenario

A company needs a professional website where administrators can maintain pages, services, news, contact information and other website content.

CMS Approach

Umbraco provides the content management layer, allowing authorized users to maintain structured website content through the administration interface.

Responsive Design

The website is designed to provide a consistent experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Custom Development

Custom templates, components and integrations can be developed to meet specific business requirements.
